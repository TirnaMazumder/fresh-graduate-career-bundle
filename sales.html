Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this book for?

Final-year students and recent graduates applying for their first full-time role, graduates switching direction, and anyone who has sent many applications without hearing back.

Is this suitable for fresh graduates?

Yes. It is written for the journey from graduation to your first job and your first 90 days.

Does it include resume guidance?

Yes: a one-page graduate structure, a bullet formula, before-and-after examples and a resume checklist.

Does it cover interviews?

Yes: preparation, the STAR method, common questions to practise, questions to ask, a thank-you email template and video interview tips.

Does it cover salary negotiation?

Yes: researching market value, answering the early salary question, negotiation scripts and what to look at beyond base pay.

Does it include practical templates?

Yes. The Toolkit includes a 30-day plan, application tracker, 30-60-90 day plan, follow-up, acceptance and decline messages, and a master checklist.

Is this a physical book or digital eBook?

It is a digital eBook.
